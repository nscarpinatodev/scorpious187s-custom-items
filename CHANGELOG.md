# Changelog

## 1.0.0

**First release.** Homebrew magic items with their own automation for dnd5e 6 on Foundry VTT 14, built for dnd5e's native rolls with Scorpious187's Luck Dice Automation.

- **Kilian's Eldritch Rifle** (in the *Scorpious187's Custom Items* compendium). Channel Spell opens a picker of the wielder's prepared ranged spell attacks. The chosen spell is cast at **double range** and **+2 to hit**, so Eldritch Blast reaches 240 ft.
- Rolls made later from the card's Attack and Damage buttons use the channeled spell too. Slots, concentration and uses are spent from the real spell.
- **Luck Dice Automation:** beams and rays, target confirmation, Luck Dice on a miss, and range checks all use the channeled range and bonus.
- **Range check** world setting: off, ask, or refuse when targets are beyond the channeled range. With Luck Dice's attack automation on, out-of-range targets stop the cast before a slot is spent.
- **Scorpious187's Module Library** is installed automatically as a dependency. It provides family logging and update notices.
